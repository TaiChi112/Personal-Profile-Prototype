# Epic: Recruiter Chatbot

## The Problem
Traditional resumes are static and passive, placing the burden on recruiters to read and parse information, leading to low engagement and missed opportunities for the candidate.

## Desired Outcome
An intelligent, interactive AI chatbot embedded directly into the personal profile that acts as a 24/7 personal recruiter, capable of answering HR questions about the candidate's experience accurately.

## Core Capabilities
- **Data Ingestion:** Ability to ingest resume data reliably.
- **Semantic Search:** Functionality to answer questions based on the ingested context.
- **Conversational Interface:** A natural, interactive chat UI.
- **Mobile Support:** Mobile-friendly UI design.

## Constraints & Environment
- Response time (Time to First Token) must be under 2 seconds.
- The AI must never hallucinate skills not present in the data.
