# Epic: Enterprise Fundamentals

## The Problem
A basic, resume-only webpage limits user engagement, lacks intuitive navigation for power users (like recruiters and tech professionals), fails to capture leads via newsletters, and falls short of legal and marketing standards required for a professional online presence.

## Desired Outcome
An enterprise-grade portfolio foundation that maximizes engagement, retains visitors through modern UX patterns (like a Command Palette and Fat Footer), captures leads efficiently, and is fully compliant and optimized for social sharing.

## Core Capabilities
- **Comprehensive Navigation:** A robust footer (Fat Footer) consolidating all critical site links.
- **Global Shortcut Interface:** A Command Palette allowing instant search and action execution from anywhere on the site.
- **Lead Capture & Engagement:** Newsletter subscription capability integrated directly with automated email dispatch.
- **Marketing & Compliance:** Dynamic SEO/OpenGraph metadata generation and strict adherence to data privacy standards (Cookie Consent).

## Constraints & Environment
- The Command Palette must feel instantaneous and not degrade page load performance.
- Email automation must rely on robust external APIs to guarantee deliverability.
- Must adhere strictly to modern data privacy standards (e.g., GDPR/PDPA).
