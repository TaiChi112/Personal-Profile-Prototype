# Data Mining Exercise 2

## 📥 Simulation Scenario

**Scenario:** We are measuring the **"Heart Rate"** of 5 people using a Smartwatch.

<Callout title="💓 Normal Behavior" type="info">
General people while resting have a heart rate of about **60-100 bpm** (beats per minute)
</Callout>

### 📊 Dataset:

| ID  | Observed Value | True Value | Context                      | Status                      |
| --- | ---

-------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| P1  | 72                            | 72                    | Normal resting                           | ✅ Normal Data                      |
| P2  | 75                            | 70                    | Resting but moved arm a bit (+5 error) | ⚠️ Normal + Small Noise             |
| P3  | 185                           | 185                   | Running away from a dog (actually tired)      | 🔵 Valid Outlier (True high value)   |
| P4  | 350                           | 70                    | Sensor malfunction (Random Error)       | ❌ Noise as Outlier (Error spike) |
| P5  | 70                            | 180                   | Running but watch is loose (-110 error)   | 🔴 Valid Data masked by Noise       |

---

## 1. 🔍 Differentiating between Noise and Outliers

### 📢 Noise

<Callout title="Definition" type="warn">
**Noise** is the **"Error"** or Random variance of data. It is the excess that we **do not want**. It distorts the true value.
</Callout>

**In the table:** It is the `+5` of P2, the `350` of P4, and the `-110` of P5.

### 🎯 Outliers

<Callout title="Definition" type="info">
**Outliers** are **"Data Objects"** that have characteristics clearly different from the majority of the data group (based on the measured values).
</Callout>

**In the table:** It is P3 (`185`) and P4 (`350`) because they jump far away from the resting group (60-100).

---
1.1 Is Noise interesting or useful?
Answer: Generally "not interesting and not useful" from a Data Analysis perspective.

Explain from data: Look at P2 (75) and P4 (350). The Error that occurred (+5 or +280) does not tell customer behavior or actual health. It is just an obstacle that makes our analysis harder.

Exception: Noise is only interesting if you are a "Sensor design engineer" who wants to study Noise to improve device quality.

### 1.2 ❓ Are Outliers interesting or useful?

**Answer:** **"Can be both"** (depending on context)

#### 🔴 Case where it's not useful:
**P4** (`350`): Is an Outlier caused by Noise → **Not useful** (must be removed)

#### ✅ Case where it's useful:
**P3** (`185`): Is an Outlier that is a true value (running from a dog) → **Very interesting and useful**

<Callout title="🎯 This is the heart of Data Mining!" type="success">
In some types of work, Outliers are what we are looking for:

- **🔐 Fraud Detection:** Unusually high credit card swipes (Outlier) are what we look for.
- **🏥 Medical:** Unusually high blood values can indicate disease.
</Callout>

### 1.3 ❓ Can objects that are Noise be Outliers?

**Answer:** **"Yes"**

**Explain from data:**
- Look at **P4** (`350`)
- True value is `70` but Noise (sensor error) caused the value to shoot up to `350`
- This `350` is considered an **Outlier** compared to friends

---

### 1.4 ❓ Are objects that are Noise always Outliers?

**Answer:** **"Not always"** (Small Noise does not make an Outlier)

**Explain from data:**
- Look at **P2** (`75`)
- True value is `70` but has `+5` Noise added
- The data is `75` which is still in normal range (60-100)
- **P2 has Noise but P2 is not an Outlier**

---

### 1.5 ❓ Are Outliers always objects that are Noise?

**Answer:** **"Not always"** (Valid Outliers exist)

**Explain from data:**
- Look at **P3** (`185`)
- This value jumps out from the group a lot (is an Outlier)
- But it is **not Noise** because the heart is actually beating fast (Valid Data)

---

### 1.6 ❓ Can Noise make correct data become abnormal data? Or make abnormal data become normal data?

**Answer:** **"Both can happen"** This is the scariness of Noise!

#### 🔴 Case A: Makes correct data → Become abnormal data (Normal → Abnormal/Outlier)

**Look at P4:**
```
Original: Resting value 70 (Normal)
  +
Noise: Sensor malfunction
  =
Result: 350 (Abnormal/Outlier)
```

<Callout title="⚠️ Consequence" type="warn">
Causes a **False Alarm** (Watch warns of heart attack even though just sitting still)
</Callout>

#### 🔵 Case B: Makes abnormal data → Become normal data (Abnormal/Outlier → Normal)

**Look at P5:**
```
Original: Running tired 180 (Abnormal/Outlier)
  +
Noise: Watch loose, cannot catch (-110)
  =
Result: 70 (Normal)
```

<Callout title="⚠️ Consequence" type="warn">
Causes a **Missed Detection** (Patient has severe arrhythmia but watch says normal, so doctor misdiagnoses)
</Callout>

---

## 📝 Key Takeaway

| Concept     | Definition                                                                              |
| ------

---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Noise**  | **"Error component"** inserted into data values                   |
| **Outlier** | **"Data point status"** that is far away from friends                    |

### 🔄 In KDD Process:

1. **We try to eliminate Noise**
   - In the Preprocessing step
   - Such as using Smoothing to clean data

2. **Then we decide on the remaining Outliers**
   - E.g. Is P3 **garbage to be thrown away**?
   - Or is it **gold (Knowledge)** that we need to mine? 💎
