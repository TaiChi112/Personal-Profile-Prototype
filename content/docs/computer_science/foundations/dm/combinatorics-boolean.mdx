# Combinatorics & Boolean (Quick Summary)

## 1. Combinatorics
Counting and arranging various patterns to find probabilities
- **Permutation:** Order **matters** (e.g., Passwords, 1st 2nd 3rd prizes) `P(n, r) = n! / (n-r)!`
- **Combination:** Order **does not matter** (e.g., Randomly picking balls, picking a team of 3) `C(n, r) = n! / (r! * (n-r)!)`
- **Pigeonhole Principle:** If there are $N+1$ pigeons but $N$ holes, there must be at least 1 hole with 2 pigeons (used to prove Hash Collisions)

## 2. Boolean Algebra
The mathematics of `0` (False) and `1` (True), widely used in Digital Logic
- **Important Laws:**
  - **De Morgan's Laws:** 
    - `NOT (A AND B) = (NOT A) OR (NOT B)`
    - `NOT (A OR B) = (NOT A) AND (NOT B)`
    - *(Trick: Push NOT inside and flip the AND/OR sign)*
  - **Distributive Law:** Can distribute variables into parentheses
- **Applications:** Designing Hardware Logic Gates and simplifying deeply nested `if-else` code
