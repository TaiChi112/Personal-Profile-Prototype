Solution/Benefit: Engineered an end-to-end Python pipeline leveraging digital image processing and Optical Character Recognition (OCR). Integrated a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) workflow to ensure high contextual accuracy and quality control in English-to-Thai translations.
